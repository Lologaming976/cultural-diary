DOCUMENT STUDIED IN CLASS

Hamilton

In class, we studied the musical Hamilton, which tells the story of Alexander Hamilton and his role in the American Revolution.

We learned about his difficult childhood, his ambition and how he became an important figure in American history.

We also discussed the musical's original casting, which uses actors from different ethnic backgrounds to represent historical American figures.

This choice challenges traditional representations and avoids whitewashing.

03

PERSONAL DOCUMENT

My Hero Academia

My personal document is the anime My Hero Academia. Specifically, I focused on the social issue of discrimination against heteromorphs (characters whose Quirks give them animal-like or non-human physical traits).

Heteromorphs often face systemic prejudice, social exclusion, and structural inequality simply because they look different. Mirroring the racism, xenophobia, and marginalization in our world. Characters like Spinner or the mutant population in general are rejected by most of the society, pushing some to radicalization.

Characters such as Shoji and Spinner help illustrate this issue and show how discrimination can affect people's lives and their place in society.

I chose this example because it brilliantly uses a fantasy world to talk about the inclusion, tolerance, and the rejection of otherness in our own world.
